
Minimal floquet photo-assisted current from Viljas and Cuevas, 2007.

    I = (e/h) int_0^{hw} dE  sum_{k,l} Tr[G(E) Gam_R^(k) G^+(E) Gam_L^(l)] (f_L^(l) - f_R^(k))

    [Gam^(l)]_{m,n} = J_{m+l}(a_W) J_{n+l}(a_W) Gam(E - lhw)
    [Sig]_a{m,n}     = sum_l J_{m+l}(eVa/hw) J_{n+l}(eVa/hw) Sig(E - lhw)

    For use with the Zandpack files used also for the nozand/zand programs.

    e -> (norb, norb) array.  Energies in eV, current in e/fs.
